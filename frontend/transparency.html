Self-audit: does the bookkeeping match the money?

The contract exposes a public auditBalance() check: it compares what it says it has raised, refunded and paid out against the ETH it actually holds right now. This isn't something the dashboard calculates for you — anyone can call auditBalance() directly on Etherscan's "Read Contract" tab and get the same three numbers, independent of this site.

How to verify this yourself, without trusting this site

1. Copy the contract address above. 2. Open it on Etherscan (link provided when on a public network) or paste it into any block explorer / your own script. 3. Call getCarnivalStats() and auditBalance() under "Read Contract" — both are public view functions, free to call, and require no wallet signature. If the numbers you get back don't match what's shown here, trust the explorer, not this page.
