What is CCN Day?

Campus Care Network (CCN) runs this carnival twice a year in the spirit of caring and sharing — bringing students and staff together to raise funds for TP students who need a helping hand.

🎪

Stalls run by the campus

Students and staff apply to run a stall — food, games, crafts — via a published eligibility list.

💳

Pay straight from your wallet

No cash, no queues at a booth. Festival-goers pay approved stalls directly in ETH from any wallet.

🤝

Funds go where they're needed

Once the carnival closes, stall earnings are withdrawn and every cent supports TP students facing hardship.

🔍

Fully transparent

Every application, approval, payment and refund is an on-chain event anyone can verify.

How it works

Four steps from application to payout.

1

Apply

Eligible students/staff submit a stall name — the site checks them against the published list automatically.

2

Approve

The organiser reviews and approves the application before it can accept payments.

3

Trade

Festival-goers connect any wallet and pay approved stalls directly in ETH.

4

Cash out

After the organiser closes the carnival, stall owners withdraw their earnings the next day.

On the day

A rough run of what to expect at the venue.

10:00 AM

Doors open

Stalls set up, approved stalls go live and start accepting on-chain payments.

12:00 – 2:00 PM

Lunch rush

Peak carnival hours — food stalls, games, and performances across the courtyard.

6:00 PM

Carnival ends

Stalls stop accepting payments once carnivalEndTime passes on-chain.

Next day

Withdrawals open

The organiser processes the close-out, and stall owners can withdraw their earnings.
